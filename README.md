# JAGUAR TEC

## Interactive Psychological Well-Being Platform for Space Missions

**Prototype for stress monitoring, emotional self-regulation, and individual and team well-being in simulated space environments.**

### Project Description

JAGUAR TEC is an interactive software prototype designed to monitor self-reported indicators of psychological stress, support emotional self-regulation, and assess individual and team well-being during simulated space missions, with potential future applications in real space environments, subject to scientific research and validation.

The platform integrates assessment tools, team monitoring, interactive concentration activities, and audiovisual resources aimed at supporting emotional well-being.

### Main Features

- **Mission Registration:** Allows users to create and manage simulated space missions.
- **Crew Registration:** Enables the registration of crew members assigned to each mission.
- **Individual Assessment:** Records self-reported indicators of stress, anxiety, fatigue, and emotional well-being.
- **Team Monitoring:** Facilitates the recording and monitoring of team communication, cohesion, and coordination indicators.
- **Galaxy Flow:** An interactive space-themed video game designed to encourage sustained attention, engagement, and concentration during gameplay.
- **Space Relaxation:** Incorporates guided breathing exercises and auditory resources intended to facilitate relaxation experiences.
- **Mission Dashboard:** Allows users to review individual and team assessment records.
- **Contact:** Provides information about the project development team.

### Scientific Purpose

The project aims to explore the potential of interactive digital tools for monitoring self-reported psychological indicators and supporting emotional self-regulation in simulated space mission environments.

Its design incorporates activities related to attention, guided breathing, and audiovisual interaction. However, the effectiveness of these interventions requires experimental studies to evaluate their outcomes.

The records generated by the platform do not constitute clinical diagnoses and are not intended to replace assessments conducted by mental health professionals.

### Project Status

**Academic research and technological development prototype.**

JAGUAR TEC is currently in a preliminary development and evaluation stage. Its functionalities are designed to explore digital tools that may support psychological well-being in simulated space environments.

At present, the project has not undergone clinical validation, and there is insufficient experimental evidence to establish that it reduces stress, anxiety, or fatigue.

The platform's functionalities, audiovisual resources, and recorded indicators will require usability testing, technical evaluation, and scientific studies before implementation in real operational environments can be considered.
